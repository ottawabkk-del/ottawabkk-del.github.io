Monthly bookkeeping for Ottawa small businesses

You run the business. I keep the books current, reconciled and ready for HST, payroll and year end.

What's included

  • Every bank, credit card and loan account coded and reconciled each month
  • Receipts and bills collected through Hubdoc or a shared folder and matched to transactions
  • Customer invoices tracked, so you know who owes you
  • Bills tracked, so you know what's coming due
  • A profit and loss and a balance sheet each month, with a short note on anything unusual
  • Questions sent to you in one email, not a stream of messages

Who it suits

Owners who are tired of doing the books at night, or who have outgrown a folder of receipts. It works for a single location or several: I keep separate books for each location of a multi-location business and report on them side by side.

How a month runs

  1. Statements and feeds come in

    Early in the month I bring in the bank feeds and statements for every account.

  2. Everything is coded and reconciled

    Each account is matched to its statement until the difference is zero.

  3. You get your reports

    Your monthly reports arrive with a short list of any questions I need you to answer.

Software

QuickBooks Online or Sage 50, with Hubdoc for receipts and Plooto for paying bills. If you already have a file, I work in it.

Other services

  • Catch-up and cleanupMonths behind, or a file nobody trusts anymore. I get it current and reconciled.
  • GST/HST returnsHST tracked through the year, returns prepared and filed with the CRA.
  • PayrollPay runs prepared for your approval, and the books matched to what was paid.
  • QuickBooks Online and Sage 50Setup, bank rules, cleanups and plain answers about the software you use.
