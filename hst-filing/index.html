GST/HST bookkeeping and filing

I track the HST you charge and the HST you pay, prepare your returns from reconciled books, and file them with the CRA.

What I take care of

  • HST collected on sales, checked against your invoices
  • HST paid on expenses, backed by receipts
  • Returns prepared and filed through the CRA's GST/HST NETFILE service
  • Payments and instalments tracked, so the balance in your books matches the CRA
  • Invoicing set up correctly for services sold to clients outside Canada

Monthly, quarterly or yearly

I file on whatever reporting period the CRA has you on. For businesses with several locations, each location's numbers stay separate so every return ties back to its own books.

Returns built on reconciled books

A return is only as good as the books behind it. Because I reconcile every account first, the figures on your return can be traced back to your bank statements and receipts.
