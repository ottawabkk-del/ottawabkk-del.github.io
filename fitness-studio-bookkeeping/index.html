Prepaid packages aren't earned until they're used

When a client buys a 20-class pack or a year of membership up front, you have the money but you haven't earned all of it yet. I record prepaid packages as they're used, so each month shows what you actually earned.

With more than one location, I track each one on its own and split shared costs like software and marketing the way you choose, so you can see how every location is doing.

Common questions

Can you report each location separately?

Yes. Each location gets its own numbers, and shared costs are split the way you choose.

How do you handle class packs and memberships?

Money paid up front is recorded as it's used, so each month shows what you actually earned.

Can you run payroll for trainers?

Yes. I prepare each pay run for your approval and match the books to what was paid.

Can you work with my accountant?

Yes. You keep your accountant. I keep your books current every month, and your accountant gets a clean, reconciled file at year end.
