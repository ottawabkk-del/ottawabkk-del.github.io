Privacy

This site uses one measurement tool, the Google tag from Google Ads, and nothing else.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send me by phone, email, the enquiry form or the booking page. I use it to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, to do your bookkeeping. I don't sell or share it.

Enquiry form

The enquiry form is run by Google Forms. What you type there is stored in my Google account under Google's privacy policy, and I get an email when you send it.

Booking an intro call

Intro calls are booked through Google Calendar. The details you enter there are handled under Google's privacy policy and come to me as a calendar invitation.

Fonts

The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Photos load from Unsplash, which also receives your IP address when the page loads.

Hosting

The web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to run the site securely.

Advertising measurement

When I run Google Ads, the Google tag on this site sets cookies so Google Ads can count when a visit from one of my ads leads to an enquiry, a booking or a click on my phone number or email. The tag doesn't collect what you type into the enquiry form. You can block these cookies in your browser or manage ad settings in your Google account.

Questions or deletion requests

Email ottawa.bkk@gmail.com and I'll answer or delete your information.
