Common questions

Can you work in my existing QuickBooks or Sage file?

Yes. If your file needs fixing first, I'll tell you what I find before doing anything.

My books are months behind. Can you still help?

Yes. A catch-up gets every account reconciled and current. Monthly bookkeeping keeps it that way.

Do I need to meet you in person?

No. Receipts can be photographed and sent through Hubdoc. Meetings are by video, or at your office when it helps.

Do you file my HST?

Yes. I prepare your GST/HST returns from reconciled books and file them with the CRA.

Do I still need my accountant?

Yes. Keep your accountant for your year-end taxes. I do the monthly books, HST and payroll, and your accountant gets a clean, reconciled file from me.

How is the fee set?

Plans start at $250 a month plus HST. You get a fixed quote before any work starts, based on your accounts, transactions and payroll.
