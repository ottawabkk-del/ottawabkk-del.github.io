Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send me by phone, email or the booking page. I use it to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, to do your bookkeeping. I don't sell or share it.

Booking an intro call

Intro calls are booked through Google Calendar. The details you enter there are handled under Google's privacy policy and come to me as a calendar invitation.

Fonts

The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Hosting

The web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to run the site securely.

Questions or deletion requests
