The GST you pay at the border can come back

Goods you import into Canada are subject to the GST or the federal part of the HST. The CRA says that if you're registered and you're the importer, you can claim an input tax credit for the tax you paid on the imported goods, as long as you meet the requirements.

I keep the customs paperwork with each order, so the credit is claimed and backed up.

Source: Canada Revenue Agency, GST/HST on imports and exports.

  • At the border

    GST is paid on most goods you import into Canada.

  • Claimed back

    Registered importers can claim it as an input tax credit.

  • Landed cost

    Freight, duties and brokerage added to what each order cost.

Common questions

Can you handle purchases in US dollars?

Yes. Each purchase and wire is recorded with the exchange, and USD accounts are reconciled in US dollars.

Can I get back the GST I paid at the border?

If you're registered and you're the importer, generally yes, as an input tax credit. I make sure the customs paperwork is on file to back it up.

Do you track inventory?

Yes. Orders are tracked from deposit to delivery, with freight and duties added, so you know what each product really cost.

Do you work with importers outside Kanata?

Yes. I work with importers across Ottawa, including Orleans, Stittsville, Nepean, Manotick and Greely, remotely or at your office.
