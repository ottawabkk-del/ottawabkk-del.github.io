Common questions

Can you work in my existing QuickBooks or Sage file?

Yes. I work in QuickBooks Online and Sage 50 files regularly. If your file needs fixing first, I'll tell you what I find before doing anything.

My books are months behind. Can you still help?

Yes. A catch-up gets every account reconciled and current. After that, monthly bookkeeping keeps it that way.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Most clients never need to. Receipts can be photographed and sent through Hubdoc, and we can meet by video or at your office when it helps.

Do you file my HST?

Yes. I prepare your GST/HST returns from reconciled books and file them with the CRA, on whatever reporting period you're on.

Will you work with my accountant?

Yes. At year end your accountant gets a clean, reconciled file, and I answer their questions directly so you don't have to pass messages back and forth.

How is the fee set?

You get a quote before any work starts. Ongoing work is priced on how many accounts and transactions you have and whether there's payroll. Catch-up work is quoted after I've seen the books.

Tell me what's going on with your books.

Call or email and I'll tell you how I can help and what it would cost, before any work starts.
