What's included

  • Every bank, credit card and loan account coded and reconciled
  • Receipts and bills collected through Hubdoc and matched
  • Customer invoices tracked, so you know who owes you
  • Bills tracked, so you know what's coming due
  • Monthly profit and loss and balance sheet, with a note on anything unusual
  • Separate books for each location, reported side by side

How a month runs

  1. 1

    Statements and feeds come in

    Send your statements and receipts by the 5th. I bring in the bank feeds for every account.

  2. 2

    Everything is coded and reconciled

    Each account is matched to its statement until the difference is zero.

  3. 3

    You get your reports

    By the 10th your books are closed and your reports arrive, with one short list of any questions.
