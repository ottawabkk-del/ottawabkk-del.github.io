Payroll for small businesses in Ottawa

I prepare each pay run, you approve it, and the books match what was paid.

What's included

  • Hours, salaries, bonuses and vacation pay entered for each pay period
  • Pay runs prepared in Wagepoint or the payroll system you already use, ready for your approval
  • Source deduction remittances tracked and reconciled
  • Payroll recorded in the books, so wages, deductions and the amount owing to the CRA all agree
  • Staff who work at more than one location split correctly between them

Why you approve every pay run

Payroll moves money out of your account, so the final approval always stays with you. I prepare everything up to that point and flag anything that looks off before you approve.
