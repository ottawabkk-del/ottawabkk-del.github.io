How pricing works

What moves my price within a plan?

How many bank and card accounts you have, how many transactions a month, how many people are on payroll, and how many locations.

When am I billed?

At the start of each month, at the fixed fee in your quote.

What if I need something outside my plan?

QuickBooks or Sage questions, one-off fixes and extra projects are quoted before I start, so there are no surprise bills.
